03 / The research method

How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet.

Keep your research simple: discover a category, record the exact item, and verify the listing before you commit.

This Oopbuy spreadsheet guide focuses on making a usable comparison list. It does not provide a live inventory feed or a downloadable product spreadsheet.

01

Choose a category and a purpose

Start with what your wardrobe needs: everyday sneakers, a lightweight jacket, a jersey, or a matching set. A clear purpose makes similar products easier to compare.

02

Build an Oopbuy product list with precise notes

Record the listing link, seller, selected variant, displayed price, measurements, and the date you checked it. Separate seller claims from details you have verified.

03

Compare fit and available evidence

Use garment measurements or foot-length guidance where supplied. Inspect available photos for visible details, while recognizing that photos alone cannot prove authenticity or durability.

04

Recheck the listing and total cost

Confirm availability, purchasing terms, and current charges. Item price is only one part of an order’s cost; delivery and other applicable charges may change your comparison.

What belongs in your comparison?

Suggested fields for an Oopbuy shopping spreadsheet.
Field What to record Why it matters
Product & source Listing URL, seller name, item description, date checked. Helps you revisit the same item and identify stale entries.
Color & size Exact variant, size chart, relevant measurements. Reduces confusion between similar-looking options.
Materials & finish Stated fabric, lining, hardware, and care information. Supports a more specific comparison than appearance alone.
Available evidence Listing photos, review context, unresolved questions. Keeps visible details separate from unverified assumptions.
Estimated cost Item price and applicable delivery, fees, taxes, or duties. Shows how the estimated overall cost compares.
Terms & status Availability, return terms, restrictions, shortlist decision. Records what still needs checking before payment.

These are suggested research fields. Prices, availability, fees, and terms must be checked against current listings and service information.

06 / Before you browse

Useful answers.
Fewer assumptions.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list that organizes product links and notes for people researching purchases through Oopbuy. The information included varies by its creator.

Does a spreadsheet confirm product authenticity or quality?

No. A product link, brand label, photograph, or spreadsheet entry does not independently establish authenticity, quality, seller authorization, or availability. Verify these details with the seller and relevant brand resources.

What should I check before using a product link?

Check the current listing, seller information, selected color and size, garment measurements, materials, return terms, and any relevant purchasing restrictions before paying.

How should I estimate the total cost?

Start with the item price, then account for any applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes, and duties. Use current quotes because charges depend on the order and destination.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial discovery page. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy or the brands mentioned.
